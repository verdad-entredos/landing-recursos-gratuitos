# Free Resource Mailbox

## Objective

Build an independent, mobile-first landing page for Verdad entre dos that presents the free resource **5 citas, 5 recuerdos** as a tactile editorial mailbox experience and provides direct, reliable access to both real PDF editions.

## Problem and why

The first free resource exists as two finished PDFs, but needs a clear standalone entry point that makes the download feel personal without adding an email gate, invented product claims, or reliance on an interaction to access the files.

## Approved concept and design

- Concept: **El buzón de los buenos momentos** — a postal/editorial experience with midnight-ink canvas, cream letter paper, a coral/cinnabar seal, fine postal rules/details, and substantial editorial typography.
- Composition: asymmetric and layered on desktop; thoughtfully stacked for mobile, not simply a scaled-down desktop or enlarged phone. An illustrated/photographic cream envelope and coral seal frame real resource artwork peeking out.
- Interaction (updated with explicit user approval): clicking or keyboard-activating “Abrir el sobre” opens a modal over a dimmed background instead of inserting the preview below the hero. Inside it, a cream envelope opens and a letter emerges with the accurate preview and both PDF links. The mobile-first dialog has a visible close control, contained keyboard focus, Escape dismissal, restored opener focus, internal scrolling when necessary, and reduced-motion support. Direct downloads remain independent of JavaScript.
- A visible primary PDF link works without opening the envelope or running JavaScript. Downloads are direct and ungated.
- Narrative: a concise Spanish headline and “recursos gratuitos” tag, short resource detail/how-to section, truthful editorial note that future resources may arrive, and small brand footer. No fake downloads, counts, testimonials, social handles, metrics, or forms. A final book-promotion module is now explicitly authorized as described below.
- Final module (user requested and destination supplied): after POSDATA and before the footer, introduce **100 citas, una historia** with the real titled book cover, three illustrated activity notes, and general relationship-oriented copy that remains relevant when more free resources are added. Headline: “Su historia merece más momentos así”; CTA: “Conoce las 100 citas”, pointing exactly to `https://100citas-unahistoria.vercel.app/` rather than checkout. Retain the postal/editorial palette, a mobile-first composition, and restrained entrance motion with a static reduced-motion/no-JavaScript baseline.
- Download wording: clarify the modal actions as “Descargar PDF” and “Descargar versión para imprimir”, retaining their existing files and download behavior. Edition names may remain as descriptive format headings, not ambiguous action labels.
- Style: rich editorial serif/system typography, local useful fonts only if their license and source are confirmed; otherwise system typography. Use existing optimized assets where appropriate; no giant flat-SVG hero, generic icon grid, required drag/hover/audio, scroll hijack, autoplay, or unnecessary 3D. Respect reduced motion and keyboard accessibility.
